# Redo of Healer (回復術士のやり直し)

**Type:** Dark Fantasy / Revenge / Psychological  
**Author:** Rui Tsukiyo  
**Illustrator:** Shiokonbu  
**Studio:** TNK  
**First Air Date:** January 2021  
**Episodes:** 12  

---

## 📖 Story Summary

*Redo of Healer* follows **Keyaru**, a healing magician whose powers are exploited by others.  
After years of torment and abuse, he discovers that his healing magic can also **rewind time**.  
Using this ability, he returns to the past to **redo his life** — this time taking **revenge** on those who wronged him.

The story deals with **trauma, morality, revenge, and rebirth**, set in a brutal fantasy world.

## ⚠️ Controversy

The series is known for its **graphic violence, sexual assault, and revenge themes**, which led to:
- **Censorship** in TV versions.  
- **Multiple releases:**  
  - *TV Broadcast Version* (heavily censored)  
  - *Redo Version* (less censored)  
  - *Complete Recovery Version* (uncensored)  
- **Bans** on some streaming platforms due to explicit content.
